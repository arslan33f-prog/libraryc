Import Stock from PDF

PDF se sirf yeh columns parhe jate hain: Item Code, Item Description, Unit, Balance (Qty banta hai) aur Avg Rate (Rate banta hai). Baaki columns chhor diye jate hain. PDF chunein ya yahan drag karein. Items aur un ki category PDF se khud pehchani jati hain (PDF ke category column ya heading se, warna item ke naam se). Preview mein check kar ke "Add to Stock" dabayein. Neeche ke box sirf tab kaam aate hain jab category na mile.
PDF mein Item Code, Description, Unit, Qty, Rate jaise columns hon to behtar parhi jati hai. Internet zaroori hai, aur scanned (tasveer wali) PDF nahi parhi jati.
